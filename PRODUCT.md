## Platform

web

## Users

"Promateur" photographers — enthusiast-to-professional photographers who grade/edit their own photos themselves, rather than delegating editing to a separate retoucher or studio team. They are the ones sitting down with a shoot's files and taking it from capture through grading to a finished image.

## Product Purpose

Qeepa is a photo workflow app for managing shoots from RAW capture to final deliverable.

## Positioning

The "tracks" system: Qeepa groups every file that shares a shot's filename but differs in filetype (e.g. the RAW capture, an exported JPEG, a graded/edited version) into a single track, so a photographer manages one linked unit across the whole workflow instead of juggling separate, disconnected files as a shot moves from capture to grading to delivery.

## Operating Context

A shoot produces multiple filetype variants of the same image over time (RAW capture → grading/export → final deliverable). Tracks are the unit Qeepa organizes around as files move through that lifecycle.

## Product Principles

- One track per shot: every filetype variant of an image (RAW, grade, export) stays linked as a single unit through the workflow, never fragmented across separate files or tools.
- Built for the person who grades their own work: the workflow is scoped to a single operator moving a shot from capture to deliverable, not a team handing files off between roles.
- RAW-to-deliverable continuity: the product's promise spans the whole lifecycle of a shot, not one isolated stage (culling, or editing, or delivery alone).
- Pre-launch discipline: until the product is public, the site's job is to earn a waitlist signup — it must not claim pricing, customers, or feature completeness the product can't yet back up.
