## Our money-back guarantee

We offer a one month money-back guarantee on your first purchase of Qeepa. If you are not satisfied, you may request a full refund within one month of your initial purchase by contacting us at [qeepaphotos@gmail.com](mailto:qeepaphotos@gmail.com).

The guarantee applies once per customer, to your first purchase only. Subsequent purchases, upgrades, or add-ons are not covered.

## How refunds are processed

Qeepa is sold through our reseller, Lemon Squeezy, which is the merchant of record for your purchase and handles payment processing on our behalf. Refunds are issued by Lemon Squeezy to your original payment method, and are also subject to Lemon Squeezy's own buyer terms.

Once a refund is issued, your licence key is disabled and your [Software Licence](/licence) ends.
